Our practice

Care begins
with a conversation.

Gonzales Dental is a family-oriented dental practice with roots in Davao City. Its story began with Dr. Cornelia “Nelly” Gonzales in 1960 and continued through her son, Dr. Dean, and granddaughter, Dr. Alyssa.

The practice’s published introduction places importance on a calm, comfortable environment, respect for appointment schedules and lasting relationships with patients.

A consultation is a chance to discuss your concerns and medical history before deciding on care. The dentist can explain the findings, whether further diagnostics are needed, and which treatment options fit your case.
